Questions & answers

Finora is still a personal/in-house build, so access is intentionally limited for now.

Is Finora public?

No. This build is invitation-only while the product flow, privacy wording, and security model are still being refined.

Where is the data stored?

The app syncs to the configured Supabase project under the signed-in user profile. Local sessions still log out when the browser session is reset.

Can I use it on my phone and PC?

Yes. Sync before editing on a device and sync after making changes. Newer builds also auto-sync on login and after saves.

Is this financial advice?

No. Finora is a tracking and planning tool only. You should still verify all figures before making money decisions.

Access is currently private.

Finora is being tested as a personal DesignLab product before any wider release.
